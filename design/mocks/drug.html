Product information

Functional groups 6

  • The β-lactam ring is why amoxicillin works. Its shape resembles the part of the cell-wall building block that the cross-linking enzyme, a transpeptidase, recognises. The enzyme's active-site serine attacks the ring's carbonyl and is left as an inactive acyl enzyme, so the wall is no longer cross-linked.

    The ring has to stay intact. Bacteria that make a β-lactamase open it, and the drug no longer binds.

What the difference teaches

Both drugs keep the β-lactam ring intact; that part has to stay unchanged for any penicillin to work. What changes is the side chain on the amide link, and changing it is how semisynthetic penicillins gained a broader spectrum.

Amoxicillin's side chain carries a primary amine, the mark of the aminopenicillins, which were developed for better activity against Gram-negative bacteria than penicillin V.
